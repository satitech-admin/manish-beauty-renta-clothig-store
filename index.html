RENTAL FAQ

Before you book.

Exact pricing, deposit, fitting and rental duration can vary by selected item, so final terms are confirmed by the store before booking.

Can I rent clothes and jewellery together?

Yes—booking form me “Clothes + Jewellery” ya “Complete look / styling” select karke requirement share kar sakte hain. Exact matching options availability par depend karengi.

Do you provide costumes for drama and school functions?

Site me drama, theatre, stage, annual day, fancy dress and cultural-program categories included hain. Character name/role notes me mention karein.

How do I know my size is available?

Size/age booking request me share karein. Store exact item and fitting availability WhatsApp/call par confirm karega.

Is the booking confirmed after sending the form?

No. Form WhatsApp enquiry create karta hai. Booking tab confirm hoti hai jab store selected item, date and terms approve kare.

Can groups or dance teams enquire?

Yes. “Group / Team” choose karke number of looks aur colour/theme notes share karein.
